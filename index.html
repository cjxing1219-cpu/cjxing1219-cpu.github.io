Anime Thai Analytics

Anime Thai Analytics is a private desktop tool used by one creator to manage their own YouTube channels of short anime commentary videos. It is not offered to the public and has no user sign-up.

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos to the operator's own YouTube channels and sets their publishing schedule.
  • Reads the upload list and file details of those channels to link each published video to the local production record it came from.
  • Reads view counts and YouTube Analytics reports for those channels so the operator can see which videos perform well.

How Google data is used

The tool only accesses the YouTube channels that the operator authorizes with their own Google account. Data stays on the operator's computer and is never sold, shared, or used for advertising. See the Privacy Policy for details.
